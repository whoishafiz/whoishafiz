Earlier research

Volume-Time Equivalence: sonoluminescence bubble collapse

Paper draft
  • Derives a volume–energy relation from mass–energy equivalence by writing mass as density × volume and the speed term as wavelength × frequency. The result is dimensionally consistent.
  • Tested against Keller–Miksis bubble-dynamics simulations, sweeping drive pressure, frequency, starting radius and gas to check that the predicted collapse timescale tracks the simulated one.
  • Maps where the relation applies: it holds where a volume truly collapses under an independently sourced energy, and reduces to an identity for linear oscillators.
